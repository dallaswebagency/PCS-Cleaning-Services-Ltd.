PCS was founded in 2009 as Provable Cleaning Services Ltd. Our clients often shortened it to PCS, so we have been operating as PCS Cleaning Services for over 10 years now.

We offer full service commercial janitorial services. In 2016 we founded PCS Property Services Ltd. to add landscaping, snow removal and building maintenance, so one team can look after your property inside and out.
